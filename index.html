BD PRO VPN – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

App Name: BD PRO VPN

1. Introduction

BD PRO VPN is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the BD PRO VPN Android application (“App”).

2. Information We Collect

BD PRO VPN does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name or email address directly through the App unless you voluntarily provide it.

However, the App and its integrated third-party services may process technical information, including device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app usage data, diagnostic information, and notification tokens, depending on the enabled features and service configurations.

3. Third-Party Advertising

BD PRO VPN uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, approximate location information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where permitted.

Users may manage certain advertising preferences through their device settings and any consent options provided in the App, where applicable.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Advertising Information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Firebase Services

The App may use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve performance. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) may be used to deliver push notifications and may process app instance tokens and related technical information.

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. VPN Services

BD PRO VPN supports VPN connections through OpenVPN, V2Ray, and SSH protocols. When you connect to a VPN server, your network traffic passes through the selected server.

We do not intentionally store users’ IP addresses or VPN connection logs on our VPN servers. Temporary technical processing may occur to establish, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot VPN connections. Any processing of connection timestamps, server selection, protocol information, or other technical data depends on the actual server configuration and operation.

We do not use such information to build user profiles or sell it to third parties.

Third-party services integrated into the App, such as Google AdMob and Firebase, may independently process technical information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

We cannot guarantee that every third-party service or external server follows the same data-retention, security, or privacy practices. VPN connections may also be subject to the policies, laws, and technical practices of the selected server provider and the jurisdiction in which that server operates. Users should avoid transmitting sensitive information through VPN servers they do not trust.

6. Data Security

BD PRO VPN takes reasonable measures to protect information processed through its services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data Sharing and Retention

Service providers may process information as necessary to provide VPN connectivity, display advertisements, analyze app usage, deliver notifications, maintain security, and operate their services.

Data retention depends on the type of information, the actual configuration of the relevant services, and the applicable providers’ policies.

We do not intend to sell users’ personal information.

8. Children’s Privacy

BD PRO VPN does not knowingly collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updates will be published on the webpage hosting this policy.
